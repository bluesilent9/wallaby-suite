01 / WALLABY SIGNER Available now

Your message.
Your signature.

Prove control of a supported Bitcoin address with an offline message-signing tool built for Bitcoin Core descriptor wallets.

  • One self-contained HTML file. No installation or server.
  • BIP-322 proofs for native SegWit, wrapped SegWit and key-path Taproot.
  • Local address matching, signature generation and proof verification.
  • Guided Bitcoin Core export steps and a copyable public signature.
Wallaby Signer on GitHub Stable V1 · Downloads, instructions & audit guidance ↗
About offline signing

Signer processes private descriptors containing xprv keys locally. Use a trusted, disconnected computer and keep exports private. Wallet passwords are entered in Bitcoin Core only. Supported policies and compatible verification formats are described in the Signer documentation. Signing a message does not spend Bitcoin.

02 / WALLABY DASHBOARDComing soon · in development

Your Bitcoin.
The whole picture.

A clearer view of wallets, transactions and family finances, with the detail to understand how it all connects.

Preview of the development roadmap. Not publicly released.

Wallets & ownership+

Import public Bitcoin Core descriptors, assign family ownership and SMSF status, and reconstruct older wallet history from owned transaction outputs. Track balances, acquisition costs and history across wallets and exchanges.

Australian CGT reporting+

Combine Bitaroo PDF statements with wallet and exchange records. Explore financial-year totals, capital gains, losses, holding periods, fee attribution and parcel allocation, with per-user reports and downloadable offline HTML working papers.

Visual transaction builder+

Plan payments with draggable wallet and destination nodes. Allocate amounts and network fees, send a wallet’s maximum, save reusable templates, prepare PSBTs, coordinate multiple owners’ signatures and download the combined result. Signing and broadcasting stay in Bitcoin Core.

Wallet Key Explorer & Address Book+

Inspect public receiving and change branches, derived addresses, balances and activity. Keep wallet information connected to the ownership records that explain it.

Fees, graphs & notifications+

Explore fee estimates and history graphs. Follow signing requests, transaction progress and data-freshness notices, with saved workflows that resume between sessions.

Vault monitoring & timing+

Import public vault QR evidence, check supported timelock policies and funding outputs, and follow estimated unlock countdowns. Explore block-height estimates using a timezone selector and interactive map.

Profiles, archives & local access+

Separate family views and preserve shared configuration, reports, records and templates in validated profile archives. Run locally with loopback access and SSH tunnels. Dashboard uses public wallet information; private keys are rejected.

Planned: Node Manager+

Direct RPC access to multiple Bitcoin Core nodes, discovery of their wallets, incremental history collection and coordinated public watch-only copies are planned. The current development version still uses descriptor imports.

03 / WALLABY VAULTComing soon · in development

Secure your Bitcoin.
Protect your family’s future.

Designed to run exclusively on a cold-storage laptop, Wallaby Vault uses Bitcoin timelocks to help protect your holdings against physical threats and coercion. By making locked funds unavailable for immediate spending—even to you—it helps reduce what an attacker could force you to transfer.

Preview of the development roadmap. Not publicly released.

Protection against physical threats+

Designed to reduce the Bitcoin an attacker could force you to transfer immediately. Timelocked funds remain unavailable before their unlock height, even to their owner. This helps protect your holdings against coercion; it cannot guarantee physical safety.

Cold-storage laptop only+

Wallaby Vault is designed to run exclusively on a disconnected cold-storage laptop alongside Bitcoin Core. Vault creation and signing stay offline. Public evidence can be transferred to Dashboard for monitoring.

Bitcoin-enforced timelocks+

Lock Bitcoin behind a spending condition enforced by the Bitcoin network. Before the chosen block height, the locked funds cannot be spent even with the wallet’s key. Use an unlock height planned with Wallaby Dashboard; calendar dates remain estimates because block arrival times vary.

Offline vault construction+

Build a dedicated timelocked vault address using a public key controlled by an existing Bitcoin Core wallet. The intended policy requires the wallet signature and the chosen block height.

Independent verification+

Guide users through checking the wallet’s public key, vault descriptor and full destination address in trusted Bitcoin Core before funding.

Funding & withdrawal workflow+

Prepare annotated Bitcoin Core commands and PSBT workflows for funding, offline signing and eventual withdrawal. Display amounts, fee settings and destinations for review.

QR transfer & Dashboard connection+

Export public vault evidence by QR for monitoring in Wallaby Dashboard. Wallaby Vault itself runs only on the disconnected cold-storage laptop; there is no online Vault mode. Private keys remain outside these public exports.

Recovery records+

Preserve the public descriptor, scripts, derivation information, unlock height and funding output details needed to reconstruct a withdrawal. The full funding-and-withdrawal workflow remains to be implemented and tested before release.

04 / WALLABY SCHOOLComing soon · early concept

An education as
individual as your child.

A future homeschooling program to help families build their child’s education program around the way they learn.

Long-term vision. Much further from release.

A program built around your child+

The core vision is to let parents shape an individual education program for each child, rather than start from a fixed, one-size-fits-all plan.

A place to bring learning together+

Proposed directions include organising subjects, learning goals, activities and resources into a coherent homeschooling plan. These are design ideas, with the detailed scope still to be decided.

Room to grow+

Future planning may explore adapting the program as children’s needs and interests change. Scheduling, progress records and curriculum alignment are not confirmed features.

Early concept · long-term roadmap+

Wallaby School is much further from release than the Bitcoin tools. It is not available to use, and there is no announced release date.
